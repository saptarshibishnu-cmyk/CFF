〜 Sinusoidal luminance modulation
Stimulus brightness is modulated sinusoidally at the target frequency using requestAnimationFrame, eliminating on/off pixel aliasing. At high frequency (50 Hz) the sine is perceived as a steady, constant light. As frequency steps down, the modulation becomes perceptible — patient presses STOP at first flicker. Mean of 2 trials = CFF score. Cutoff: < 39 Hz = MHE.
